Why shippers choose RootNode Fold

Container-level capacity

Three modules join to match a 40 ft container, so loads ship as they would in any standard box.

Foldable return trips

Empty boxes take up space and money on the way back. Folded units pack down to about 1.2 m thick.

Built for heavy cargo

Curved joints and internal ribs spread load instead of concentrating it at the corners.

Standard handling

Works with standard forklifts and pallet systems, and stacks up to three high when folded.

Folding takes minutes, not hours

Unfolding is the same process in reverse. Crews need no special equipment.

  1. Release the locks

    Open each quick-lock one by one.

  2. Fold the side panels

    Hinged side panels swing in.

  3. Fold the top panels

    Hinged top panels close down onto them.

  4. Secure for transport

    Strap the stack or lock the frame, then load it.

The RootNode joint

Inspired by how trees branch, the RootNode joint blends beams into a smooth curve instead of a sharp corner. That reduces stress concentration, and internal vertical and horizontal ribs carry load through the central core and into the frame.

Quick-lock in three actions

Align on the guiding surface, slide and lock with the cam mechanism, then verify with the visual indicator.

Green: lockedRed: unlocked
